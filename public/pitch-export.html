01 / Context

Friends live in one app. Discovery lives in another.

Instagram kept the people you know. TikTok took the camera. Every creator, and every teenager, now runs two lives — and two brands — to do both.

02 / Problem

We trained a generation to split themselves.

The close graph stalled

Follow graphs optimized for ads, not friends. Chronological intimacy is an afterthought on platforms built for reach.

The open graph is a stranger

Short video found a new camera language — and left your actual Circle behind. Discovery without belonging.

Identity is the tax

People maintain two handles, two aesthetics, two audiences. The software made them do it.

03 / Insight

The bet

Closeness and discovery are different verbs.

One ranking function cannot serve a best friend and a billion strangers. The fix is not a better algorithm. It is two rooms, one name.

05 / Circle

Circle

The feed that used to exist.

People you follow, in the order they posted. Captions that can be a paragraph. Images that do not autoplay. A home, not a slot machine.

Follow, unfollow, block — a real social graph
Posts: text, image, or video
Like, comment, share, save
Hashtags without turning friends into SEO

06 / Pulse

Pulse

A public square that does not invade the house.

Full-screen vertical video. Swipe. Sound. Reach that is not gated by how many friends you collected in high school.

Same username, separate ranking
For You, following, and tags
Create once — publish to Circle, Pulse, or both
Friends never compete with strangers in the same scroll

07 / System

A dual graph, not a feature flag.

01 Identity — one user, one username, hashed credentials, sessions.
02 Graph — follow edges that power Circle, never Pulse ranking.
03 Content — posts and media with a room, not a generic blob.
04 Signals — likes, comments, saves, shares, notifications.
05 Safety — report, review, suspend. Trust is the product.

Not another For You page. A social OS with two rooms.

09 / Market

The camera already split. The software has not.

2 lives

Every young user already keeps a friends app and a discovery app.

1 camera

Short video is the default way a generation records the world.

0 homes

No major product is natively both — so people duct-tape identity.

Social remains the largest consumer-internet surface. The opening is not “beat TikTok at TikTok.” It is to own the seam between belonging and broadcast.

10 / Model

Monetize the square. Protect the house.

Pulse is public, so it can carry ads, branded missions, and later, creator commerce. Circle is a home. We do not rank friends for a brand manager.

Pulse advertising — native, skippable, never in Circle
Creator tools on Pulse reach, once the graph is real
Never sell the follow graph. Trust is the lock-in.
